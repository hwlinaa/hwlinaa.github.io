David Hin Wang Lin

I am a PhD student in Electronic and Computer Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Ling Shi. I conduct research at the Cheng Kar-Shun Robotics Institute and the Multi-Agent Systems Laboratory, focusing on UAVs, marine robotics, and motion planning and control.

I co-founded Universpirit Innovation with Prof. Shi and fellow team members, developing aircraft teaching kits and aviation courses for schools. I also co-founded NaviHK Tech, a technology consultancy, and serve as President of the Hong Kong Science Talent Society, contributing to technology education and youth development.
